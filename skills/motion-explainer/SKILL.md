# Motion explainer

Create and render a polished motion-graphics explainer of the user's topic. Handle art direction, implementation, dependencies, sound design, and refinement end to end.

Work in the requested project directory. Use React/TypeScript and Remotion for animation. Python audio synthesis and FFmpeg can help with sound and media preparation.

Restructure scenes and add components or dependencies as needed. Prioritize clarity over spectacle.

For a detailed execution brief, adapt [production-prompt.md](references/production-prompt.md). The [fictional document-review example](references/process-explainer.md) supplies a complete topic without external assets.

## Format

Default to 45–60 seconds, 1920×1080, at 60fps unless requested otherwise.

## Creative direction

Make it elegant, calm, and clear. Use restrained typography, a cohesive palette, and readable copy.

Where useful, carry an object, example, or layout into the next scene and transform it to explain what happens next.

Use layout transformations, masks, camera reframing, and motion curves to clarify the action. Avoid an animated slide deck of repeated cards, caption bars, arrows, and fades, or decorative motion that adds no meaning.

## Story

Explain the requested topic or sequence clearly. Shape the scenes around the main takeaway and what the viewer needs to understand.

## Assets and facts

Use supplied assets where relevant and create visuals suited to the topic. Keep recurring examples recognizable and preserve the meaning and composition of supplied content. Label conceptual illustrations. Do not invent evidence, measurements, or results.

## Sound

Unless requested otherwise, create a restrained original synthesized score and subtle effects synchronized to meaningful actions. Use a shared cue timeline for picture and sound.

## Review and delivery

Render and inspect representative scenes and key transitions. Fix visible problems before the full render. Watch playback and listen when tools permit; stills alone cannot establish pacing or sound quality.

Deliver:

- The MP4.
- Editable source and reproduction instructions.
- Concise creative notes.
- Verification of duration, frame count, dimensions, color, full decode, and audio timing/levels when audio is present.

Report the checks performed and any review still needed. Compilation alone does not mean the film is finished.

## Example reference

You may watch [this motion explainer](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pjr_U-lLN8tP6xqgwIvJn3tQ7iAVOF3H/view?usp=drivesdk) for ideas about pacing, transitions, and sound. Adapt the techniques to your subject.
