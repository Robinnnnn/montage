# GoPro inputs

Use the standard video workflow for compatible flat MP4 footage. Before a large import, scout and render samples from each camera model and recording mode. Play them to check color, crop, motion and sound.

1. Record each source file’s identity, duration, dimensions, frame rate, color metadata and original capture time. Keep originals unchanged and record the chosen source ranges.

2. Review split chapters as one recording, keeping each file and recording which ranges you use. Joining chapters is not required for review.

3. Compare capture timestamps and time zones against known shared events across cameras. Record clock corrections and uncertainty. Filenames and download modification times are not reliable capture-time evidence.

4. Scout across the complete recording, then inspect promising intervals and neighbors closely. Widely spaced sample frames can miss brief action; use playback to check motion, sound and boundaries. Save review notes for later revisions.

5. Choose the canvas before cropping. Inspect people, action and wide-angle context before cropping landscape footage vertically. Check color for each camera and recording mode: a supported phone HLG conversion is not a universal GoPro preset. Resolve unsupported HDR or incomplete color tags before rendering; retagging is not conversion.

6. Spherical/360 footage needs a separate reframing workflow that produces reviewed flat video first. Finding an MP4 file does not mean the helpers can reframe 360 footage.
